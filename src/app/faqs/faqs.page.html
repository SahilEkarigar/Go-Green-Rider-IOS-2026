FAQs
Frequently Asked Questions

What is Go Green?

Go Green is a platform connecting eco-conscious customers with sustainable vendors and products.

How do I register as a vendor?

Go to the vendor section and click on “Register.” Fill in your store details and submit for verification.

Can customers order directly from vendors?

Yes, customers can place orders directly from the vendor profile if the vendor has enabled that feature.

How do I locate eco-friendly stores near me?

Enable location access and use the “Find Stores Near Me” option on the home screen.

How can I add my store’s exact location on the map?

While registering, use the map picker to drop a pin or auto-detect your store’s location.

Is Go Green available in all cities?

Currently, Go Green is live in select cities. We are expanding rapidly—stay tuned!

How do I update my store information?

Go to “Profile” > “Manage Store” and update your details. Changes may require admin approval.
